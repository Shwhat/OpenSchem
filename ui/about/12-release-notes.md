# Release notes

The installed version is shown at the top of this guide.

## 1.22.0 (2026-11-17): About guide

- About OpenSchem guide in the Help menu and the account menu: overview, capabilities, a page per area, technical details and these release notes. It works without a network connection.

## 1.21.0 (2026-11-17): Decks

- Decks attached with a ledger, freestanding or at ground level, with L-shapes and levels joined by steps.
- Joists, beams, posts and footings suggested from the AWC DCA 6 tables for southern pine, Douglas fir-larch, hem-fir and spruce-pine-fir, with warnings for sizes over the tables.
- Decks from New page, from a room or a house wall on a floor plan, or from a building wall. Linked decks follow their room or wall.
- Low decks hang the joists from flush beams and set the beams on piers.
- Decking in 5/4x6, 2x6 or composite, laid square, diagonal, with a picture frame border or a breaker board, with joints over joists.
- Stairs, steps between levels, landings, handrails and guards with balusters.
- Footing and post plan, framing plan, decking plan, elevations and stair detail pages; building views, lumber list, cut list, cutting plan and hardware list.

## 1.20.0 (2026-11-17): Tile layouts

- Tile layout pages for floors and walls from a floor plan room, a floor plan wall side, a framing wall or a typed outline, kept linked to the source.
- Doors, windows and cabinet or fixture blocks are left out; walls take a height range; areas can be added or cut out.
- Straight grid, running bond, herringbone, basketweave, hexagon and versailles patterns.
- Start point, balanced cuts and smallest cut, with thin cuts shown in red and chalk lines dimensioned from the walls.
- Tile cut list and tile order report pages, and Excel export.

## 1.19.0 (2026-11-17): Splicing

- Plates, rim joists, ridge boards and skids longer than the longest board are spliced over a stud, joist or rafter.
- Rafter ties are lapped at the middle or butted with a scab.
- Splice marks on the drawings and "part 1 of 2" in the cut list.
- New building no longer refuses a building with a lumber problem.

## 1.18.1 (2026-11-16): Building views fix

- Fixed: shell walls in the building views showed steps and seams, and doors and windows did not line up in their openings.

## 1.18.0 (2026-11-16): Building views

- Building views report with framing, finished shell and exploded framing, seen from the four corners.
- The 3D window switches between Shell, Framing and Exploded for buildings.

## 1.17.0 (2026-11-16): Framing

- Framing pages for small buildings and single framed walls, from New page or from a room on a floor plan.
- Doors and windows dragged along the wall or typed in Properties.
- Floors on skids, gable and single-slope roofs, sheathing and roof panels.
- Lumber list, cut list, cutting plan and Excel export, in North American or metric lumber.
- Make floor plan from a building, and a 3D view.

## 1.16.0 (2026-10-30): Floor plan drawing

- Fixed: the rectangle tool did not place a rectangle when ortho was on. Ortho is now off by default.
- Press, drag and release as well as click and click on every draw tool.
- Rectangles and polygons stay true shapes with their sizes in Properties.
- Dynamic input beside the cursor (F12).

## 1.15.0 (2026-10-29): Standards and pneumatics

- Fixed: a selected floor plan dimension was selected by clicking anywhere inside it.
- IEC 60617 and NFPA 79 symbol libraries, single-line symbols, and conversion between standards.
- Part numbers made of the manufacturer code and order number, and a shared manufacturer list.
- Single-line and pneumatic pages, an ISO 1219 pneumatic library, tubes and the tubing list.

## 1.14.0 (2026-10-28): Server version

- OpenSchem runs as a server in Docker for amd64 and arm64.
- Accounts, projects on the server, sharing, one editor at a time, per-user preferences, autosave and shared libraries.

## 1.13.0 (2026-10-28): Dialogs, panels and custom reports

- Dialogs are ready for typing and Enter confirms.
- Panel docking improvements, an open to-dos filter and a New page split button.
- Circuit legend object, symbol editor that works like a page, driving dimensions, report settings and order, and custom reports.

## 1.12.1 (2026-10-28): Ribbon reset

- A new version resets the saved ribbon so new buttons appear.
- Align to grid on floor plans covers walls, doors, windows, shapes, free dimensions and blocks.

## 1.12.0 (2026-10-28): Floor plan drafting

- Units and drawing scale, object snaps, ortho and polar, typed input, command line, dockable panels and workspaces.
- Draw and edit tools, walls, doors, windows, rooms from walls and view sheets.
- Dimensions, dimension styles, leaders, hatches and blocks with attributes.
- DXF import and export, SVG and PNG export.

## 1.9.0 (2026-10-27): Navigator, parts and panel layouts

- Page navigator with thumbnails, zoom window, display and user layers.
- Parts database with costs, load calculation, voltage drop and breaker labels.
- Page graphics, black boxes, device boxes, PLC cards, Excel import and export, find and replace, terminal strip editor, panel layouts and paper sizes.

## 1.4.0 (2026-10-27): File format versions

- Project files record their format and program version.

## 1.3.1 (2026-10-27): First release
