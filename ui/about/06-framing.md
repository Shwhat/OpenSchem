# Framing

## Buildings and walls

- New page > Building or Framed wall, or Building from room on a floor plan (a rectangular room with its doors and windows).
- Walls are seen from outside. The front and back walls run the full width; the side walls fit between them.
- Each building gets a framing plan, one elevation per wall, floor framing and roof framing.

| Part | Choices |
|---|---|
| Walls | Stud length, stud size, spacing, single or double top plate |
| Openings | Doors and windows with rough opening, sill and header (flat or doubled) |
| Floor | Joists on skids, treated or not, decking |
| Roof | Gable or single slope, pitch, rafters, overhangs, rafter ties |
| Sheathing | Wall and roof sheets, roof panels down the slope |

## Lumber

- North American or metric lumber, set in Project properties.
- The lumber list uses the fewest boards from the lengths turned on in the building's lumber settings.

## Splicing

| Run | Joint |
|---|---|
| Plates, rim joists, ridge boards, skids | Butt joint over a stud, joist or rafter |
| Cap plates | At least 24" (600 mm) from the top plate joints |
| Rafter ties | Lapped at the middle, or butted with a scab |
| Rafters, studs, floor joists | Never spliced |

- No spliced piece is shorter than the shortest piece set for the building.
- Splices are marked on the drawings and listed as "part 1 of 2" in the cut list.

## Building views

- A report per building with framing, finished shell and exploded framing, each seen from the four corners.
- The 3D window switches between Shell, Framing and Exploded.
