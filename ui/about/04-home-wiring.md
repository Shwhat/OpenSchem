# Home wiring

## Circuits and breakers

- In a home project the function is the circuit and the location is the room.
- Breakers have a slot, amps, poles, type and panel. The panel schedule lists them by slot.
- Devices placed on the floor plan take the room they are in.

## Loads and voltage drop

| Value | Worked out from |
|---|---|
| Circuit load | The devices on the circuit, with editable defaults per kind, and the LOAD attribute of blocks |
| Percent of rating | The load and the breaker amps. Over 80% is a project check warning. |
| Voltage drop | Wire size and run length |

## Floor plan wiring

- Circuit colors and switch legs are drawn automatically.
- A circuit legend is added from the Floor plan tab.
- Place on schematic opens the device's circuit page, or a page for its room.

## Breaker labels

- Breaker labels print as a panel directory in the two-column door layout, on a letter page or a 4 x 6 in label.
