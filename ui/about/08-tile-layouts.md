# Tile layouts

## Surfaces

| Source | Tiled area |
|---|---|
| A floor plan room | The floor, less cabinets, tubs, showers and appliances (tile runs under a toilet) |
| A floor plan wall side | The wall, less doors, windows, openings and blocks against it |
| A framing wall | The inside or outside face, less doors and windows |
| A typed outline | The outline as typed |

- A layout follows its source. If the source is deleted, the layout keeps its last outline and says so.
- Walls take a height range, for example a backsplash from 36" to 54".
- Areas are added or cut out on the tile page and stay in place on the source.

## Patterns

| Pattern | Tile size |
|---|---|
| Straight grid | Width and height, any rotation |
| Running bond | Offset of 1/2 or 1/3 of a tile |
| Herringbone | The long side is the width |
| Basketweave | The long side is the width |
| Hexagon | Width across the flats |
| Versailles | The small square; four sizes in each module |

## Start and cuts

- The layout starts from the middle of the area, a corner or a picked point, with a joint or the middle of a tile on it.
- Balanced cuts try a joint and a tile middle on each direction and keep the layout with the fewest thin cuts.
- The smallest cut is 1/3 of a tile unless set. Thinner cuts are shown in red.
- The page shows the starting tile and the chalk lines, dimensioned from the walls.

## Reports

| Report | Contents |
|---|---|
| Tile cut list | Each cut's size and shape, equal cuts grouped, straight cuts taken from offcuts where they fit |
| Tile order | Full and cut tiles, tiles to order with waste (10%, or 15% for diagonal and herringbone), boxes, grout and thinset estimates |
