# Decks

## Making a deck

| Start from | Result |
|---|---|
| New page > Deck | A typed rectangle, L-shape or two levels |
| A room on a floor plan | A deck that follows the room's size |
| A house wall on a floor plan | A ledger deck along the wall |
| A building wall elevation | A ledger deck along that wall |

- Kinds: attached with a ledger, freestanding, or ground level.
- Sections at one height side by side make an L-shape. Sections at different heights are joined by steps.
- Each section has one height over flat ground.

## Sizes

- Joists, beams, posts and footings are suggested from the AWC DCA 6 Prescriptive Residential Wood Deck Construction Guide (2012 IRC edition), for southern pine, Douglas fir-larch, hem-fir and spruce-pine-fir.
- The tables assume 40 psf live load, 10 psf dead load and No. 2 grade lumber.

| Member | Rule |
|---|---|
| Joists | The smallest size that spans (Table 2), with a cantilever within the table and a quarter of the span |
| Beams | The fewest posts, then the smallest beam (Table 3A) |
| Posts and footings | 6x6 posts and footing size by beam and joist span (Table 4) |
| Ledger | 2x8 or larger, lag screws or bolts at the Table 5 spacing |
| Low decks | Joists hang from a flush beam; the beam sits on the piers when there is no room for a post |

- Sizes can be changed. A size over the tables is kept and listed as a warning on the page, in Properties and in the project check.
- Suggestions follow the tables. The local building department has the final say.

## Decking

| Decking | Joist spacing (square / diagonal) |
|---|---|
| 5/4x6 wood | 16" / 12" |
| 2x6 wood | 24" / 16" |
| Composite | Set per deck, 12" or less when diagonal |

- Layouts: square to the joists, diagonal, picture frame border (with blocking), breaker board (over doubled joists).
- Butt joints land over joists, are staggered at least two joists apart, and every board bears on three or more joists.

## Stairs and guards

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Riser | 7 3/4" or less |
| Tread | 10" or more |
| Width | 36" or more |
| Cut stringers | 18" or less apart, three or more, spans of 6' or less |
| Guards | Where the deck is over 30" above grade, 36" high, openings under 4" |

## Output

- Pages: footing and post plan with stake-out dimensions, framing plan, decking plan, elevations and stair detail.
- Reports: building views, lumber list, cut list, cutting plan and hardware list.
