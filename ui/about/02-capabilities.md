# Capabilities

This page lists what OpenSchem does in the current version, followed by what is planned.

## Available now

### Schematics

- Schematic, single-line and pneumatic pages on A2, A3, A4, Letter, Tabloid or a 3 x 5 in note card, each with its column grid and title block.
- Devices tagged =Function +Location -Device, with automatic device, contact and wire numbering, contact mirrors and cross-references.
- IEC 60617 and NFPA 79 symbol libraries, single-line symbols, residential symbols and an ISO 1219 pneumatic library with tubes.
- Black boxes, device boxes, PLC cards, terminals, cables with conductors, macros and a symbol editor.
- Find and replace, terminal strip editor, display and user layers, revisions and a to-do list.

### Home wiring

- Circuits and breakers with panel schedules, load calculation, voltage drop and printable breaker labels.
- Devices shared between the schematic and the floor plan, with switch legs and a circuit legend.

### Floor plans

- Levels in feet and inches or metric with a drawing scale.
- Drafting tools with object snaps, ortho, polar, dynamic input and a command line.
- Walls, doors, windows, rooms, dimensions that follow the geometry, driving dimensions, hatching and blocks with attributes.
- View sheets, DXF import and export, SVG and PNG export, and a 3D view.

### Panel layouts

- Enclosures, DIN rails, wire ducts and device footprints drawn to scale from the parts database.

### Framing

- Buildings with gable or single-slope roofs and single framed walls, with doors, windows, floors on skids and sheathing.
- Splicing of long runs over supports, lapped or scabbed rafter ties.
- Lumber list, cut list, cutting plan and building views (framing, shell, exploded).

### Decks

- Ledger, freestanding and ground level decks, L-shapes and levels joined by steps.
- Sizes suggested from the AWC DCA 6 tables for four species, with warnings over the tables.
- Decking layouts, stairs, guards, footing and post plan, framing and decking plans, elevations, stair detail and a hardware list.

### Tile layouts

- Floors and walls from floor plan rooms and walls, framing walls or typed outlines, kept linked.
- Six patterns, start point, balanced cuts, smallest cut, tile cut list and tile order.

### Reports and output

- Cover sheet, table of contents, panel schedules, bill of materials, terminal diagrams, connection list, cables, tubing list, door and window schedule, revision overview and the framing, deck and tile reports.
- Custom reports, PDF export with bookmarks and links, Excel and CSV export, Excel import.

### Server

- Accounts, projects kept on the server, sharing, one editor at a time, autosave and shared libraries.
